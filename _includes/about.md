Hi, I'm Tim. I'm a biologist and machine learning researcher. I work at
[Open Athena](https://www.openathena.ai/), a nonprofit developing open AI models for science. I'm currently working on [MarinFold](https://github.com/Open-Athena/MarinFold), where we are training a language model to predict protein structure from sequence alone. Last year, I worked on [BoltzGen](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.20.689494v2), a protein design model.

Much of my career has centered on the immune system and cancer. At [Mount Sinai](https://icahn.mssm.edu/) in NYC, I spent a few years as a senior scientist at the [Tisch Cancer Institute](https://icahn.mssm.edu/research/tisch). Along with [Alex Rubinsteyn](https://rubinsteyn.com/) and [Julia Kodysh](https://github.com/julia326), I launched an initiative called [OpenVax](https://github.com/openvax) to create an open bioinformatics platform for personalized cancer vaccines. We deployed our software in [four](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02721043) [Phase](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03223103) [I](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03359239) [trials](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05010200) led by [Nina Bhardwaj](https://icahn.mssm.edu/profiles/nina-bhardwaj). I also did a PhD at Mount Sinai with Uri Laserson. My [dissertation](/pdfs/TimODonnell-dissertation.pdf) introduced new methods for T cell epitope prediction, which we implemented in the [MHCflurry](https://github.com/openvax/mhcflurry) package. This grew out of earlier work in [Jeff Hammerbacher's group](https://github.com/hammerlab). After Mount Sinai, I worked at [Imprint](https://www.imprint.org/), a [focused research organization](https://www.convergentresearch.org/about-fros) that is trying to decode immune receptor repertoires. I also spent two years [consulting](https://www.inferenceprojects.com/) for biotech companies on machine learning.

At the start of my career, I built analysis software for molecular dynamics simulations at [D.E. Shaw Research](https://www.deshawresearch.com/) and worked as a software engineer at several web startups. I hold a B.S. in Math & Computer Science from [Brown University](https://cs.brown.edu) and a PhD in Immunology from the [Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai](https://icahn.mssm.edu/).
